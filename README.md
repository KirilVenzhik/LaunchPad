# LaunchPad
Space launch schedules are fragmented and unstable (subject to delays, scrubs, or holds) and are tied to UTC. People interested in watching a launch often miss the event or find out about a postponement too late.

The core is not a catalog, but a reaction to changes. Any aggregator can "show a list of launches." The engineering and product value lies in reliably capturing a reschedule and promptly communicating it to the right people.
